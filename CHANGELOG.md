# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-07
- Several lines, each with its own train, colour, timing and approximate-route note (`lines:`).
- Train styles: `steam` (with smoke) and `fast` (a sleek train in the line's colour).
- `scale` per line, for a smaller train on a short line.
- Stations: `stop: false` shapes a curve without a dot; `label: left` puts the name on the left.
- Second line: Palmerola to Campo Marte (the 2026 Utopia map), approximate for now.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-07
- First-class wagon (golden, three windows, a tiny wifi signal and an AC snowflake on the roof), set by `train.first_class`;
  the engine, centring and smoke follow the longer train.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-07
- A small train travelling a railway line north to south, from a digitized GeoJSON line or a smooth
  curve through stations; stations light up as it passes; smoke; play/pause; reduced-motion support;
  provenance.json.
